    Oxide analysis

    Fe3+ is estimated with Droop (1987) only for minerals with a fixed ideal cation total; biotite and amphibole are reported with all Fe as Fe2+ because their vacancy and OH contents make charge balance underdetermined.

    Batch processing

    One row per sample. Columns are named Phase_Oxide (e.g. Grt_FeOt, Liq_H2O, Trace_Ti_zrc) plus Sample, P_kbar and T_C. Unrecognised columns are listed, not guessed. Every equation whose phases are present is evaluated.

    Drop a CSV or Excel (.xlsx) file here or click to choose

    Comma, semicolon or tab separated text, or the first sheet of an .xlsx workbook · processed locally. A table with one analysis per row (oxide columns, optionally Phase and Sample) is also accepted: you confirm the phase of each row first.

    Implemented equations

    Every equation is implemented directly from its original publication and checked coefficient by coefficient against the printed equation, including variable definitions and units; test/core.test.js re-checks them. “Pending” = implemented from the paper, awaiting that check; excluded from ensembles. Further calibrations are being added as they are re-implemented from their original papers.
